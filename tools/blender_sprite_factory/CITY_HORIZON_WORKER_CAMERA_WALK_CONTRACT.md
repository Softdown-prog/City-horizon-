# City Horizon worker: camera and walk contract

This Blender-production contract does not alter SDL3 projection, road geometry or
NavigationNetwork. It is subordinate to `CH_CAMERA_V1`.

## Frozen camera

- Orthographic projection; perspective disabled.
- 2:1 dimetric ground diamond.
- World yaw: 45 degrees.
- Camera elevation: **30 degrees**.
- Camera and lights remain fixed; the actor root rotates for each view.
- Ground origin is the sole anchor. Every direction and every frame must retain
  the same projected origin.

The prior 35.264-degree wording was true-isometric metadata and is retired.
It must not be used by new Blender scenes.

## Canonical visitor walk V1

- Eight logical directions: `n, ne, e, se, s, sw, w, nw`.
- Eight deterministic frames per direction.
- One canonical model and action drive all 64 final frames.
- Conservative stride, limited arm swing and no frame-to-frame ground-anchor
  motion.
- Runtime integration is through `TYCOON_CHARACTER_SEQUENCE_V1` plus a
  `CH_ACTOR_CONTENT_PACK_V1` generated by
  `tools/tycoon_photo_studio/character_catalog_ingest.py`.

A sequence remains a visual candidate until its generated review board is
approved at gameplay scale.
