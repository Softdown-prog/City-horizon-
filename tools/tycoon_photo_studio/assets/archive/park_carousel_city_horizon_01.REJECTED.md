# park_carousel_city_horizon_01 — rejected visual direction

Status: `REJECTED_REFERENCE_LIKE_DO_NOT_PROMOTE`

The V1–V5 visual design is preserved only as development history. It must not be promoted to runtime or used as the visual basis for the production City Horizon carousel because it remained too close to the external reference and was not visually successful.

## What may be reused

Only neutral geometric/mechanical information may be reused:
- 3x3 footprint
- base/platform radii and vertical proportions
- canopy envelope and construction topology
- central rotor hierarchy
- support spacing
- two-ring ride-mount layout
- general mechanical separation between static foundation and rotating assembly

The canonical neutral starting point is:

`tools/tycoon_photo_studio/assets/templates/carousel_geometry_template_v1.json`

## What must not be reused as design language

Do not carry forward the previous:
- yellow/cream roof stripe treatment
- burgundy/teal/gold palette
- valance/frieze treatment
- medallions and rosettes
- concentric roof trim
- mirrored center drum styling
- horse styling
- horse topper
- decorative base panels
- previous boarding-step appearance
- previous color-mask layout

The next production carousel must establish an original City Horizon visual identity from the neutral geometry template rather than iterating on V5.
